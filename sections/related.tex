The bench reuses GEO's prompt and word share~\cite{aggarwal2024geo}, and our tests follow the counterfactual style of C-SEO Bench and What Gets Cited, which edit one page and hold the rest fixed~\cite{puerto2025cseo,vishwakarma2026cited}.
Our vocabulary finding fits earlier ones: edits that made a page more relevant to the question made models side with it more often while stylistic edits did not~\cite{wan2024conflictingqa}, and a page holding the query's terms won the first citation in four of six models~\cite{vishwakarma2026cited}.

Among open tools, aiseo-audit keeps an evidence tier for every factor and demotes lists and tables to unscored diagnostics~\cite{aiseoaudit}, and Citation Equity adapts code from it; geo-score keeps measured citation apart from its readiness score~\cite{geoscore}.
What is new here is a writing tool rebuilt from its own controlled tests, including tests of the questions a writer can actually know.
