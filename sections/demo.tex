\emph{Station 1, the 90-second loop.} The visitor clicks \emph{Try it with an example}: four reader questions, a draft that covers none of them, and one blocker on the example page.
Two typed edits, a few words in the title and one answering sentence, take the count to 2 of 4; the visitor then applies the ready edit through Preview, reads its label, and opens the recorded before-and-after test beside its chance line.
A live run (64 answers, 15 minutes) starts each session and fills in while visitors watch.
\emph{Station 2, your readers' questions (offline).} Visitors type their questions or keywords and paste their own paragraph; they see which questions it covers and which words are missing, and the text never leaves the laptop.

\emph{Station 3, can engines reach your site? (online).} The command-line \texttt{ce site} checks the visitor's URL for blockers over a phone hotspot; offline, pasted HTML and robots.txt run the same checks in the browser, except those that need the live site.
