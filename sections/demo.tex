\emph{Station 1, the 90-second loop.}
The visitor clicks \emph{Try it with an example} and sees four reader questions, a draft that covers none of them, and one blocker on the example page.
The ready edit adds a question mark to a heading, common GEO advice, and its chip reads \emph{No effect in our test}; applied through Preview, it leaves the count at 0 of 4.
The visitor then opens the example's recorded before-and-after test beside its chance line: its after draft followed the old checklist and rose within noise (cited in 56\% then 66\% of answers; share 0.09 then 0.14 against 0.125, $p = 0.25$).
A live run (64 answers, 15 minutes) fills in during each session.
Last, two typed edits, a few of the reader's words in the title and one answering sentence, take the count to 2 of 4 (Figure~\ref{fig:workspace}).

\emph{Station 2, your readers' questions (offline).}
Visitors type their questions or keywords, paste their own paragraph, and see which questions it covers and which words are missing; the text never leaves the laptop.
