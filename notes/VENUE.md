# The Web Conference 2027, Demo track: requirements (read 2026-10-24 from https://www2027.thewebconf.org/demos/)

- Single PDF, at most 4 pages INCLUDING references. Double column, English.
- Template: ACM acmart, `\documentclass[sigconf, screen, review]{acmart}` (Word: ACM Word Interim Template).
- NOT anonymous (single-blind): list author names and affiliations.
- MANDATORY: a section on the ethical use of data and/or informed consent of research participants.
- MUST outline how the demonstration would be instantiated and deployed at the conference venue.
- Must be based on an implemented and tested system. External material (code repo, demo video, web system) highly encouraged.
- Submission: OpenReview, Demo track. Deadline 2026-11-16 AoE; notification 2027-01-04; camera-ready 2027-01-31.
- Review criteria: originality, significance, quality, clarity.
- Accepted demos: presented in person with a poster on site; each paper needs its own registration by camera-ready.
- AI-generated content policy: not stated on the page (check OpenReview and the ACM policy before submitting; the AI-use statement is the owner's).

## Judgement calls for fitting 4 pages (owner: "not a typical ML paper; use your judgement")

- This is a systems/demo paper: lead with what the tool does and why it matters to creators; the measurement work is supporting evidence, not the headline method.
- Figures: Figure 1 (overview + headline) and at most one results figure; everything else as one compact table or a sentence.
- Related work: one tight paragraph with the most relevant citations; no survey.
- Experiments: the evidence grading, the measurement lessons, and the pre-registered sensitivity result, each in a few sentences; E1 as a short design paragraph with a placeholder for its result.
- Required sections that cannot be cut: Ethics, Demonstration at the venue.
