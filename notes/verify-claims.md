## C. Minor accuracy and consistency items

1. **Score name.** The paper's "checklist score" matches neither the shipped UI ("GEO Score": `CE/README.md` lines 19, 34, `presets.ts`) nor the redesign ("AI-ready score": `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-dashboard-redesign-design.md` line 22). A reviewer who opens the demo sees another name. Add "(shown as the GEO Score)", or adopt the redesign name once it is deployed.
2. **"not a prediction" against "not a forecast".** The prose quotes the shipped label "a writing checklist, not a prediction" (presets.ts lines 21 to 23). The Fig. 1 schematic (`fig1_overview.tex` line 134) uses the redesign's "not a forecast". Pick one and match it to the deployed UI on submission day (the system.tex comment already says to recheck).
3. **Bench prompt.** "answers ... under GEO's prompt" (system.tex): the bench keeps GEO's instruction word for word and adds one reminder line after the sources (`CE/samples/README.md` line 49; sensitivity.py header). Write "under GEO's prompt plus one format reminder", or "adapted from GEO's prompt".
4. **Chance level in the laptop rerun.** "every 95% interval ... included the 0.125 chance level" is true of all four intervals as numbers. But for the espresso before draft, which was retrieved in 24 of 32 runs, the even-split level is 0.094, not 0.125 (`CE/samples/README.md` line 90). The sentence holds; a footnote-level precision only if space allows.
5. **Length of the after drafts.** "14% and 22% longer" uses `wc -w` (831 to 946, 1,041 to 1,275; re-run today, matches). The engine's own word counts in `data/scores.json` give +9% and +19% (823 to 897, 1,045 to 1,240). Both are traceable; state the counter in the comment (already done) and keep it consistent if the counts move into a figure.
6. **RAG after score in the workspace.** The paper's 45 to 76 uses the draft's own headings as targets. With the pack's four questions, as the workspace sets them for the samples, RAG after scores **77** (`CE/samples/README.md` line 94). Station 1 uses espresso (unchanged at 35 to 73), so the demo is fine. A visitor who opens the RAG sample will see 77.
7. **Pre-registration timing.** "the plan was committed (3c86c02) 24 minutes before the results (4d77fa0)" matches git (20:18:42 and 20:43:05 PDT, 24 min 23 s). Commit order alone does not show that the jobs started after the plan. Qwen3.5-9B alone took 1,103 s to load plus 186 s to generate, about 21.5 min, so the four jobs must have run in parallel and started within about 2 min of the plan commit. If space allows, cite the SLURM start times from the Explorer job logs. Also, `sensitivity.py` changed after the plan only by a 7-line licence header (`git diff 3c86c02 HEAD`), which is worth one comment line.
8. **Bajemon and Rochet.** "re-measured the original GEO edits": the abstract says "the only published causal anchors (2023 effect sizes) on ten modern engine families". The link to Aggarwal et al. comes from corpus R39, not the abstract. "the 2023 GEO effect sizes" is the safer paraphrase.
9. **AnchoredAI.** "preserved writers' agency in a lab study" is accurate (a within-subjects laboratory study, 22 university students; significant on 4 of 5 agency and ownership items). The paper found *more* agency than a chat interface, so "improved" is the more faithful verb.
10. **CE README is stale** (out of scope for the paper, but reviewers will read it). `CE/README.md` line 57 says "37 to 72 and 45 to 76", against 35 to 73 (foundation) in `samples/README.md` line 94 and `data/scores.json`. 37 is espresso's *evidence*-preset before score, and no preset gives 72. Fix it in the tool repo before submission.
